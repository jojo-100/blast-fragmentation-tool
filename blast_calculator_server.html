The trained model predicts rock factor A from the five properties, then fills the Rock factor (A) box in the inputs above.
Method. Mean fragment size from the Kuznetsov equation as reformulated by Cunningham: X50 = A*(V0/Q)^0.8*Q^(1/6)*(115/RWS)^(19/30), where V0 = B*S*H is rock volume broken per hole. Spread from the uniformity index n = (2.2 - 14*B/d)*sqrt((1+S/B)/2)*(1 - W/B)*(L/H). Size distribution from Rosin-Rammler: P(x) = 1 - exp(-0.693*(x/X50)^n). Rock factor A: ~7 soft/weathered, ~10 medium, ~13 hard jointed, up to ~22 massive/strong. RWS = explosive weight strength relative to ANFO (=100).

References: Cunningham, C.V.B. (1983), "The Kuz-Ram model for prediction of fragmentation from blasting"; Cunningham (1987), 2nd Int. Symp. on Rock Fragmentation by Blasting. Validation cases carry their own published sources (shown per case). This is a planning estimate; the model assumes a single explosive type and does not capture fines from the crushed zone or in-situ block effects.
