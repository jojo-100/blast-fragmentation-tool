Compare Against Real Mine Data

About these field cases. MO-1–DI-5 are 10 real sites at the Sungun Copper Mine (Gheibie et al. 2009, Int. J. Rock Mechanics & Mining Sciences 46:967–973). The measured sizes and rock factors come from that paper. The paper did not publish the blast geometry, so the geometry here is reconstructed — the comparison is made against the measured field size. Across all 10 sites, Kuz-Ram under-predicts the mean size by about 17–25%. That gap is a known limitation of the model, not an error in this tool.

How it works. Mean fragment size: X50 = A*(V0/Q)^0.8*Q^(1/6)*(115/RWS)^(19/30), with V0 = B*S*H (rock volume per hole). Curve shape: n = (2.2 - 14*B/d)*sqrt((1+S/B)/2)*(1 - W/B)*(L/H). Size distribution: P(x) = 1 - exp(-0.693*(x/X50)^n). Rock factor A guide: 7 = soft, 10 = medium, 13 = hard jointed, 22 = massive/strong.

References: Cunningham (1983, 1987), the Kuz-Ram fragmentation model. Field cases: Gheibie et al. 2009 (shown per case). This is a planning estimate: it assumes one explosive type and does not capture crushed-zone fines or in-situ block effects.
